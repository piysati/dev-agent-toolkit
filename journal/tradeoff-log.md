# Trade-Off & Architecture Decision Log

> **Purpose**: Top 1% engineers are defined by judgment under constraints. Log every significant design, work, or learning trade-off you evaluate—what you chose, what you rejected, and what would make you flip the decision.

```markdown
- **Context & Constraints**: <Traffic, latency, team size, consistency, budget>
- **Baseline / Naive Option**: <Simplest approach and where it breaks>
- **Options Weighed**:
  | Option | Pros | Cons / Risks |
  |--------|------|--------------|
  | Option A | ... | ... |
  | Option B | ... | ... |
- **Decision**: <Chosen option and core reason>
- **Reversal Trigger**: <What metric or requirement change would make us switch?>
```
